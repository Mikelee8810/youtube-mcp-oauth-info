Mikelee8810 YouTube Connector — Personal Connector

This is an independent, self-hosted connector that lets its authorized owner access and manage their YouTube channel through ChatGPT using the YouTube Data API.

It runs on the owner's computer. Google OAuth is used to request the owner's explicit permission. Depending on the tools the owner invokes, it can read channel information, playlists, videos, subscriptions and comments and perform authorized actions such as managing playlists or ratings. Actions are not taken without the owner's request.

This connector is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google, YouTube or OpenAI.
